Our services

Nine services, organised around the three things we set out to do.

Building peace

  • ADR and conflict mediation

    Neutral, confidential mediation that helps parties settle disputes without going to court — for families, workplaces, communities and businesses.

  • Marriage counselling

    A calm, private space for couples to talk through difficulties and work towards understanding.

Developing people

  • Corporate training and staff development

    Training programmes built on a proper needs assessment, so teams learn what actually improves their work.

  • Manpower counselling

    Guidance for staff and employers on career paths, morale, conditions of service and workplace concerns.

  • General HR services

    Practical support with HR administration, welfare and staff records.

  • Human capital management

    Helping organisations recruit, develop, motivate and keep the right people.

Strengthening organisations

  • Organisational development

    Reviewing structures, policies and performance so your organisation runs more effectively.

  • Security consultancy

    Security risk assessment, security policy design and review, and staff security training.

  • Safe school consultancy

    Helping schools plan and coordinate measures that keep pupils and staff safe.

Ways to work with us

  • One-off sessions for a mediation, counselling session or single training
  • Projects with a defined scope, such as a security review or staff training programme
  • Retainer and advisory support for ongoing HR, security or organisational guidance

Managing Director

Comdt. Olufunke O. Adebisi, Ph.D (Rtd)

Dr. Adebisi spent over two decades in the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC), rising to Commandant (Administration) at NSCDC Zone 8 Headquarters, Makurdi. Her career covered field command, intelligence and surveillance, staff welfare and administration across Kwara and Benue States.

She holds a Ph.D in Political Science (Public Administration) and an M.Sc in Peace and Conflict Resolution, and is an MTI Accredited Mediator. PeaceGate brings that experience to families, schools and organisations that need fair, practical help.
